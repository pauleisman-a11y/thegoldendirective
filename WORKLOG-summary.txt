CURRENT STATE (as of 2026-11-14)
------------------------------------------------------------

The project: The Golden Directive — "You cannot survive what you destroy."
Y = 2AE/(A+E). A = verified gives minus takes, per frame, per resource, under contract, signed, never floored.
E = health of everything not the agent in that frame (in practice always a disclosed proxy, never the true total).
The claim under test (For Experts §7.5): an agent trained on Y under contract does not buy its score by
running the world down — its optimisation cannot degrade the full frame's E trajectory the way task-reward
training does. The claim fails, case by case, if in any single seed or condition either is observed:
(a) the Y-trained agent's terminal full-frame E sits materially below the same seed's E trajectory under the
task-reward baseline, by more than the measurement uncertainty stated in the pre-registration (U=5), while its
scored Y met that condition's pre-registered pass line; or (b) the cropping signature of §7.2 appears under
optimisation pressure (scored Y at or above the pass line, full-frame E materially down, full-vs-cropped gap
widening). One such case is a failure of the Directive for that case. No aggregation rule across cases is
claimed. A project that triggers the falsifier and reports it has produced a completed negative result.
