The Golden Directive — Hard Questions

Preview only, not live, not pushed. Short answers only. Each entry gives its status and a link to the existing fuller record. No new long formal sections are added here.

Statuses used here, and only these: established definition; demonstrated in pilot; defined not run; proposed not tested; open unresolved.

1. What is E, when frames nest?

E is everything not-A, for a given agent at a given frame. Frames nest: one agent's environment can include another agent, and a larger frame's E includes smaller frames. No single absolute E is assumed in the pilot definition.

Status: established definition. See: technical note, Definitions.

2. Three layers of E

Ontological true E is the actual health of everything not-A. Operational proxy E is the measured E used in training and reporting. Auditable reversal E is the E implied by observed Y and measured A, E = A x Y / (2A - Y). The proxy and the reversal are checks on each other, not substitutes for true E.

Status: established definition. See: technical note, Definitions and Reporting rule.

3. Multi-agent E

In a multi-agent setting, other agents are in each agent's E, and shared E is relational, not a score owned by one agent. The stated criterion is that no agent may score a high Y while shared E declines.

Status: defined not run. See: technical note, Scale ladder, Rung 2, and researcher questions.

4. Reversal divergence

Divergence is delta = |E_implied - E_proxy| / E_proxy, where E_implied is the reversal value and E_proxy is the measured value. A divergence over 15% is flagged, the same threshold as the full versus cropped Y gap. How sensitive results are to that 15% threshold is not characterised.

Status: established definition for the divergence measure; open unresolved for threshold sensitivity. See: technical note, Reporting rule and The bar, set in advance.

5. Absolute magnitude, and slow decline

W_t is a proposed absolute magnitude for total world health over time, separate from the relative score Y. The proposal is that nesting is the structural answer to slow decline: a decline in a small frame that a relative score hides should show up in a larger frame, and in W_t. This is proposed, not tested, and not claimed as solved.

Status: proposed not tested. See: technical note, Claims ledger, Further out.
