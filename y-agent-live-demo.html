Scanning the local field for the weakest living patch.
Y = 2AE ÷ (A + E)

A high contribution cannot cover a ruined world. The lower side pulls the score down.

Y = 2AEA + E

The harmonic mean makes imbalance expensive. If either side approaches zero, the shared reward follows it.

The agent is not told to be good.

It is given a different grade. A is what the agent contributes. E is the health of everything it is not. Y is the strength of the relationship between them.

In this visual model, the Y-agent seeks the weakest patch and tends it. Switch to Extract only to see the same little world under a reward that counts only what the agent takes.
