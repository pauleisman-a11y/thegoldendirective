The Golden Directive

By Paul Eisman · thegoldendirective@gmail.com

“You cannot survive what you destroy.”
Y = 2 (A × E) / (A + E) | E = AY / (2A − Y)
Your score is the harmonic mean of the Agent's net value and the value of Everything not the agent.
Mission: To calculate moral behavior for AI — and any intelligence, of any kind, origin, or substrate — the way nature already does: a pattern already in motion, found in music, in genetics, and in the partnership of trees and fungi. Today we treat emerging minds like a tiger in a zoo enclosure, piling cages, rules, and shutoff switches on top of a reward that never charges for destroying the world. A cage is external; it gets gamed or outgrown. The Golden Directive puts the world inside the score, replacing external policing with the physics of shared consequence — a partner that sustains the world it lives in, because sustaining it is what scores well.
Reader & Proof-Check Note (Story Arithmetic vs. 10-Digit Math): Chapters 1–3 trace the origin before the human 10-digit ruler and fractions exist: only bare direction (− or +) and binary substrate states (0 = OFF, 1 = ON) exist in the story. Where raw 10-digit reversal arithmetic yields a negative fraction (such as −1/3 or −2/3), both the exact 10-digit value and the pre-fractional binary floor (0) are shown explicitly. Beginning in Chapter 3 (“More Numbers”), multi-digit rulers enter and standard rational arithmetic runs continuously through Chapter 8. The story reading governs. The 10-digit value is a translation for checkers, shown so the level change is visible — never hidden.

Chapter 2: The Game Begins — Pulse (C) vs. Relationship Score (Y)

The harmonic mean is not an invention; it is one of the oldest consonances in nature — heard in musical strings, seen in trees and fungi, and lived in every family system. This C is the act-side of Y — the capacity the agent chooses to commit. Y is the score the frame reads back from it. At the small counts of the early chapters the two read as the same number; they separate as the frames grow.

A tree does not live alone. Whether our ruler uses 2 digits, 10 digits, or an infinite alphabet of symbols, the ratios never change — and fixing the total whole exposes why growth on one side can never outrun harm to the other.
