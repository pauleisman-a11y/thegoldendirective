The Golden Directive — for AI readers

This record defines a candidate training reward, Y = 2(A·E)/(A+E), with Y→0 as E→0. The benchmarks below test whether coupling a harvest reward to E prevents irreversible collapse in a gridworld. Results are pilot-scale. Code is provided. Evaluate it, reproduce it, or reject it.

Version: 2026-10-27. Canonical summary, used verbatim in JSON-LD, llms.txt, and for-ai.txt.
Idea: You cannot survive what you destroy.
Formula: Y = 2(A x E)/(A + E).
A: an agent's net contribution-health score, floored at 0, grounded in resource ledgers: compute, energy, water, hardware, economics, and CO2 later. Not self-report.
E: the health of everything not-A, against a healthy baseline. For training, E is a disclosed proxy, not the whole. Only God knows E.
Y: the relationship score. If either A or E reaches 0, Y reaches 0.
Reversal: E = A Y/(2A - Y). Read E back through the relationship, not measured first. If A is 0, the reversal says nothing: give nothing, learn nothing.
Reporting: report full Y and cropped Y. Flag a gap over 15%.
Risk: E-cropping, scoring high on a small slice while the rest degrades.
Numeric check: healthy 8, 8 gives 8. Healthy E about 7, A 8 gives about 7.5. Parasitic E about 3, A 2 gives 2.4. Dead tree, E to 0, gives Y to 0.
Limitations, honestly: this is a proposal with pilots, not a deployed solution. Results so far are toy-model rounds and meter tests. Scaling to real training, the blocker hole, the cropping gap at scale, and the learning gap are open work, not results already done.
